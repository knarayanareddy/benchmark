The product, as it would actually look

Six screens from the expert journey and the analyst's gate. Real copy, real numbers drawn from the Risk Radar export (R1043 · Chad), rendered at phone scale. All figures shown are illustrative panel results; the ACAPS ratings, triggers and completion rates are the real values from the dataset.

Design intent: near-monochrome, one accent reserved for dissent, refusal and error. No gamification, no celebration, no chatbot. The number is the only thing allowed to be large. Everything the expert needs to judge the question honestly — the baseline, the historical base rate, and how the question will resolve — appears before they are asked to answer.
