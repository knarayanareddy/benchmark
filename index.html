Why Ground Truth Circle Solves the ACAPS Bottleneck

Addressing the 4 structural challenges in crisis intelligence elicitation:

1. 90-Second Delphi Protocol

Replaces 45-minute unstructured phone calls with low-friction, high-frequency interval forecasting cards tailored by country and thematic domain.

2. Give-to-Get Anti-Groupthink

Neither ACAPS ratings nor peer forecasts are revealed until the expert commits their Round 1 estimate, guaranteeing cognitive independence.

3. 4-Tier Differential Privacy

Named, Alias, Sealed (known only to ACAPS), and Full Anonymous tiers with retroactive anonymity retreat protecting field personnel.

4. Dissent Routing Loop

When expert dissent identifies a flaw in the risk trigger or definition, private impact receipts close the loop directly back to the contributor.
